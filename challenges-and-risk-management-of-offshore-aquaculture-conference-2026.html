Challenges and risk management of offshore aquaculture

Xueliang Wen, Muk Chen Ong, Yiliu Liu

Jakarta, Indonesia · December 11–12, 2025

Abstract

Offshore aquaculture has emerged as a promising response to the challenges faced by nearshore aquaculture under global warming, offering deeper, colder, and better-exchanging waters for sustainable salmon production. However, fully exposed offshore environments introduce new engineering and biological challenges. This review examines key challenges, including cage structural integrity, station keeping, and fish welfare, and outlines a risk management framework for offshore aquaculture development and operations. By consolidating current knowledge, identifying critical gaps, and highlighting potential solutions, this study provides guidance for advancing the sustainable development of offshore aquaculture.
