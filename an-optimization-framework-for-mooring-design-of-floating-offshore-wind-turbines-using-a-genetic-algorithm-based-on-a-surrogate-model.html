An optimization framework for mooring design of floating offshore wind turbines using a genetic algorithm based on a surrogate model

Xueliang Wen, Guang Yin, Tianhui Liu, Muk Chen Ong, Chao Wang, Kai Wang

2025

Abstract

Summary of the published abstract.

This study develops an automated approach to reduce mooring system mass for floating offshore wind turbines while satisfying dynamic performance requirements. SIMA simulations provide turbine motions, hub accelerations, mooring tension coefficients, and touchdown lengths under rated and extreme conditions. A Kriging surrogate predicts these responses, allowing a genetic algorithm to optimize mooring configurations efficiently. A three-step procedure progressively adjusts the design variables and their ranges to improve surrogate accuracy and reduce simulation costs. The optimization achieves mooring mass reductions of 12.5%, 17.1%, and 18.0% relative to the initial design. Wind farm simulations further indicate that wake effects can make designs optimized for an isolated turbine conservative for downstream turbines.
